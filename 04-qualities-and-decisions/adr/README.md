## Template

```markdown
# ADR-NNN: Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
What architectural problem or trade-off are we addressing?

## Decision
What did we decide and why?

## Consequences
Positive and negative impacts (coupling, modifiability, availability, performance).
```
